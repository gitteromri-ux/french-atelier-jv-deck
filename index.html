03 · New product line

Cultural Capsules: language through culture, as a product.

30 live one-hour conferences on Zoom, each followed by 15–20 minutes of Q&A with a bilingual signature speaker. No French required, sold in packs of ten, independent of the language courses. Method: les petites histoires qui font la grande Histoire, every subject tied back to France–US links.
